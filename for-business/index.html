Hire people who are ready to work

Flow Corner helps South African job-seekers get CV-ready. If you are hiring, tell me about your roles and I'll help you reach them.

Tell me about your role See how we can work together
How it works for employers
Three simple steps
1. Tell me what you need
Send the role, the province and how soon you need someone. It takes about two minutes.
2. I review and agree a plan
I get back to you within 24 hours to agree how the role is shared and what, if anything, it costs.
3. Your role gets seen
Approved roles are shared with job-seekers who use Flow Corner's free tools and guides.
Why Flow Corner
Built around job-seekers, so you meet them where they are

Flow Corner started as free career help for South Africans: CV checks, a CV builder, interview practice and an opportunities board. The people here are actively working on getting hired. If you have a role to fill, or a team that could use career development, this is a place to reach them.

Flow Corner is new, and I'd rather be straight with you than oversell it. Every employer enquiry is handled by me personally.
