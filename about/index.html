My background spans HR and administration, progressing from HR Assistant through to Generalist and into operations and leadership support. As an AI Projects Manager I run AI adoption projects (voice bots, chatbot integration and automation strategy), which gives me a hands-on, practitioner's view of how AI is reshaping modern workplaces, not just a theoretical one.

What I want is simple: for capable people to stop getting filtered out because of a weak CV or a LinkedIn profile nobody can find.
