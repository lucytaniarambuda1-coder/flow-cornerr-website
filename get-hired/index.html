Questions
Good to know
Do I need to pay for an AI tool?

No. Everything works with the free versions of ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Microsoft Copilot.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. The guide and prompts are PDFs, and the CV templates open in Word or Google Docs on your phone or computer.

Will AI make things up on my CV?

Not if you follow the kit. Every prompt tells the AI never to invent experience, and the guide shows you how to check what it writes.

I don't have much experience. Will it help me?

Yes. There's a prompt and a section for first CVs, plus learnerships, internships and graduate programmes for people who are starting out.

Can you do my CV for me instead?

Yes, that's a separate paid service. A CV Revamp starts from R100 and a LinkedIn Revamp from R150.
